How triggering works: the webhook is the real trigger - the sender's own tool (a mail-forwarding rule, Apps Script, Zapier, or curl) posts the message, so Consilium never holds anyone's mailbox credentials. Every inbound - including a rejected one - is written to a hash-chained, tamper-evident audit ledger before the council convenes; open the ledger to watch the chain verify (and flip red if tampered). Gmail / Outlook OAuth are deliberately left as skeletons for forks. Recommend-only: a trigger convenes and flags specialists, it never auto-executes.
